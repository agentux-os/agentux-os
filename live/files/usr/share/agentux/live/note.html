Welcome to AgentUX Live

Nothing here touches your disk. To keep AgentUX, use Install AgentUX (desktop or panel; needs internet).

The demo: the cockpit (Meta+A) shows two runs in ~/agentux-demo. They are played by fake agents, so no vendor login is needed. Approve the plan of “Add a /health endpoint” in the inbox and watch it go through plan, implement, checks, review and pull request.

In a terminal: aux ps, aux watch <run>.

Real agents: Install the agent CLIs on the desktop downloads them into RAM; an installed system sets them up at first login.
