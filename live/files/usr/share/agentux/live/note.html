Nothing here touches your disk. To keep AgentUX, use Install AgentUX (desktop or panel; needs internet).

In a terminal: aux ps, aux watch <run>.

Real agents: Try the agent CLIs in this session (application menu) downloads them into this session's memory (RAM); they are gone when you reboot. An installed AgentUX installs them automatically at first login.
