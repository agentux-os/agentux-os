## Highlights

- **Meet the penguin.** The cockpit and the Welcome wizard have a helper: a guided tour on first use, a *What's this?* mode that explains any part of the cockpit you click, and *Ask*, which answers questions about AgentUX offline and, if you allow it, with your own agent ([agentux-desktop 0.5.3](https://github.com/agentux-os/agentux-desktop/releases/tag/v0.5.3), [ADR 0017](https://github.com/agentux-os/agentux/blob/main/docs/adr/0017-help-mascot.md)).
- **Hyprland live ISOs, tested.** Their live sessions are now booted and checked in a VM, in UEFI and BIOS, like Plasma's, and their ISOs ship with every stable release.
- **Verified desktop configuration.** The images are composed from agentux-desktop release bundles, checked against their published checksums, (common, Plasma, COSMIC) instead of loose files.
